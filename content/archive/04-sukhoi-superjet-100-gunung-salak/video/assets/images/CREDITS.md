## Note on scope

No photo of the 9 May 2012 crash site, wreckage, or victims is used. The
aircraft shown is a Sukhoi Superjet 100 of the type, not the accident
airframe; none of the images depicts RA-97004 on its final flight. Mountain
photos show Gunung Salak generally, not the impact location.
